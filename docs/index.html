FAQ

Before your first breath

Is Fala free?
Yes. Box breathing, the longer exhale, today’s breath, your meadow and the First week program are free. Fala Premium unlocks every technique, your own rhythm, all programs, full statistics and Apple Health.
How long does a session take?
Most sessions take two to five minutes. Three minutes a day is enough to make it a habit, and you can always go longer.
Can I breathe with my eyes closed?
Yes. Gentle haptics follow the rhythm, so you feel each inhale, hold and exhale without looking. Soft phase sounds are optional.
What is night mode?
A dark session screen for the evening. The controls stay hidden until you tap and sounds are off by default. You can turn it on automatically from 21:00 to 6:00 in Profile.
Does Fala work with Apple Health?
Yes, with Fala Premium. Turn on Apple Health in Profile and every finished session is saved as mindful minutes. Fala only writes to Apple Health and never reads from it.
Does my data leave my iPhone?
No. Your sessions, ratings and settings stay on your iPhone. Only your subscription status is checked with Apple and our purchase provider. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.
Is breathing practice safe for me?
Breathe comfortably and stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable. Fala is a wellbeing app, not a medical device. If you have a heart or lung condition or are pregnant, ask your doctor first.
Which iPhones and languages?
Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Fala speaks English and Polish.
